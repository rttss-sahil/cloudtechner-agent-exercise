# payments-api: Too Many Connections

## Overview
payments-api shares the checkout cluster's Postgres. 'Too many connections' errors follow long-draining transactions during high volume.

## Symptoms
ERROR: too many connections; payments-api 503s; settlement jobs fail.

## Steps
1. Check connection pool and long-running settlement transactions.
2. Increase pool limits after confirming baseline.
3. Kill stale sessions from failed settlement retries.
4. Raise alert thresholds so retry storms drain.
