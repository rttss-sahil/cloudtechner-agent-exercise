# Customer Communication During Incidents

## Overview
Guidelines for communicating with customers during incidents. Customers are informed via the status page and email for S1/S2 incidents.

## Symptoms
Communicating an ongoing incident to customers.

## Steps
1. Publish status-page update within 15 minutes of S1/S2 declaration.
2. State impacted services, start time, and next update time.
3. Post an update every 30 minutes until resolved.
4. After resolution, send a postmortem summary email within 24h.
